The building

A slender landmark in the heart of the city

Spire rises on the corner of Plein Street and Spin Street, beside Church Square in the cultural and political centre of Cape Town. Curved balconies and a pale blue façade give a compact city site a building of real presence.

The apartments are compact by design. Efficient plans keep purchase prices accessible and rental returns strong, while the upper floors look out to Table Mountain and Devil's Peak.

  • Balconies or Juliet balconies to most apartments
  • Plunge pool and roof terrace on the 17th floor
  • Reception, lift lobby and coffee shop at street level
  • Optional furniture packages and air-conditioning

Investment

Two tax allowances in an investor's favour

Spire is a new residential building inside Cape Town's Urban Development Zone. Buyers who let their apartment, or otherwise use it in a trade, may qualify for one of two allowances under the Income Tax Act.

UDZ

Section 13quat of the Income Tax Act

Urban Development Zone allowance

When you buy a unit in a new UDZ building from the developer, 55% of the purchase price is treated as the qualifying cost. That amount is deducted from taxable income over 11 years.

  • 20% in the year the apartment is first used for trade, such as letting
  • 8% a year for the following 10 years
  • Not ring-fenced, so any excess can reduce your other taxable income

Example on a apartment

Qualifying cost (55%)
Year 1 deduction (20%)
Years 2 to 11 (8% a year)

13sex

Section 13sex of the Income Tax Act

Residential unit allowance

Investors building a rental portfolio can use Section 13sex instead. It applies to new and unused residential units that are let within South Africa.

  • 5% of the cost deducted each year
  • For a unit bought from a developer, 55% of the purchase price is treated as the cost
  • You must own at least five qualifying new units, which need not all be at Spire

The two allowances cannot be claimed on the same cost. Your tax adviser will confirm which suits your position.

This is a general summary, not tax advice. The UDZ incentive is currently legislated for buildings brought into use by 31 March 2030, and allowances already claimed are recouped if the apartment is sold. Neither allowance applies to an apartment used only as your own home. Please take advice from a registered tax practitioner on your circumstances.
