Assumptions

How the figures are worked out

  • Gross rental income is the nights in the month, multiplied by the projected occupancy and the average nightly rate.
  • The average nightly rate is what the owner receives after the booking site's commission.
  • Per-stay costs assume an average stay of three nights.
  • Net rental income is gross rental income less the expenses in the table.
  • Not included: levies, rates and taxes, bond repayments and income tax.

Disclaimer

Read this before relying on the figures

Estimate only

This projection is an estimate only and is not guaranteed. Your actual earnings may be higher or lower than the projections provided. The projections are based on various assumptions, including but not limited to average daily rates, occupancy levels, property location, and market conditions. These assumptions may change and can have a significant impact on the projected earnings. Please consider this information as a starting point for your own due diligence and research.

Input drivers

The input drivers for this earnings projection are based on current information and past performance of similar properties in the area and market. We use market data, industry trends, and local real estate conditions to estimate average daily rates and occupancy levels.

Expenses

The expenses included in our earnings projection are based on our current cost structure and the costs that we are currently aware of. This cost structure is subject to change.

Net profit

Net profit is calculated by subtracting the specified expenses from the projected revenue. The operating costs and revenue are based on the factors explained above. It is important to note that the net profit projections are subject to the same limitations and uncertainties as the operating cost and revenue projections.
